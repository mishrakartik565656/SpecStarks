About Us

We are second-year students participating in Spectrum 2026, organized by the BCA third-year students of the Dr. Virendra Swarup Institute of Computer Studies.

Our Project: SpecStarks

SpecStarks is a citizen-focused platform designed to help create cleaner, safer, and more responsible communities.

What We Do

SpecStarks enables citizens to report cleanliness-related issues, request waste pickup, and track the progress of their complaints. It also helps administrators review reports, verify clean-up activities, and manage public-service requests efficiently.

Our Purpose

We created SpecStarks to make communication between citizens and local authorities simpler and faster. Our goal is to encourage civic responsibility and contribute to cleaner, healthier neighborhoods.

Key Features

Solving real-world waste problems with smart, verified workflows.

📸

Easy Reporting

Citizens can snap a photo of dumped garbage. The app auto-detects the location to create a verified report.

✅

Smart Verification

Admins verify issues and assign them to workers. Before and after photos ensure proper cleanup.

🏆

Reward System

Both citizens and workers earn points for successful cleanups, encouraging active participation.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I report a waste issue? +

Simply log in to the Citizen Portal, click "Report Waste", upload a photo, and the app will automatically capture your location. Submit the report and track its progress!

How do I earn points? +

Citizens earn points when their reported issues are successfully cleaned and verified. Workers earn points for completing assigned cleanup tasks.
