Waste Segregation Guide

Proper waste segregation is the first step towards a sustainable city. Learn how to sort your waste.

🟢

Wet Waste (Biodegradable)

  • Vegetable & fruit peels
  • Leftover food
  • Eggshells
  • Tea leaves / coffee grounds
  • Garden waste (leaves, twigs)
🔵

Dry Waste (Recyclable)

  • Paper & Cardboard
  • Plastic bottles & containers
  • Glass bottles
  • Metal cans & foils
  • Rubber & thermocol
🔴

Hazardous / E-Waste

  • Used batteries
  • Paints & chemicals
  • Old electronics (phones, wires)
  • Medical waste (syringes)
  • Broken glass & bulbs
